### hi. these are my main projects and just things i've worked on and decided to upload.

**[uncertainty-guided-active-learning](https://github.com/anizakaryan/uncertainty-guided-active-learning)**
Active learning on CIFAR-10 with ResNet-18. Compares random, least-confidence, margin, entropy and MC-Dropout BALD sampling; learning rate and weight decay tuned with Bayesian optimization.

**[spotify-tracks-classification](https://github.com/anizakaryan/spotify-tracks-classification)**
Rock vs. non-rock classification of Spotify tracks from audio features using Random Forest and Logistic Regression.

**[telecom-churn-statistical-analysis](https://github.com/anizakaryan/telecom-churn-statistical-analysis)**
Churn rates by contract type, payment method and services for 7,043 customers; 95% confidence interval and one-sided z-test in R.

**[OpenCV image processing practice](https://github.com/anizakaryan/opencv-image-processing-exercises)**
Color spaces, filtering, edge detection, thresholding, blob and face detection.
